Entry Guide & Security Rules

Please follow these steps upon your arrival at the arena gates:

  1. Have your digital or printed QR pass ready for scanning.
  2. Pass through security bag inspection (bags must be under 30x30 cm).
  3. Collect your custom RFID festival wristband at the counter.
  4. Proceed directly to your designated zone or seating tier.
