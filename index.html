Entry Guide & Security Rules

Please follow these steps upon your arrival at the arena gates:

  1. Have your digital or printed QR pass ready for scanning.
  2. Pass through security bag inspection (bags must be under 30x30 cm).
  3. Collect your custom RFID festival wristband at the counter.
  4. Proceed directly to your designated zone or seating tier.

Frequently Asked Questions

Glass containers, professional DSLR cameras with detachable lenses, fireworks, and sharp objects are prohibited at gate checkpoints.

Tickets are fully transferable through your digital pass portal, but are non-refundable within 14 days of the festival opening date.

The Golden Circle and Mosh Pit are strictly 16+. Attendees under 16 must remain in seated grandstand sectors with a guardian.
